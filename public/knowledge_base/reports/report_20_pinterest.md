# Heuristic Evaluation Report: Pinterest

**Evaluator:** AIxHE Auditor
**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Framework:** Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics (NNgroup)

## Executive Summary
Pinterest excels at visual discovery and aesthetic design. However, structural navigation and the process of organizing saved items suffer from usability issues.

---

## Identified Violations

### 1. Board Organization Complexity
**Heuristic Violated:** #7 Flexibility and efficiency of use
**Severity:** 3 (Major usability problem)
**Description:** Moving multiple pins from one board to another, or into a new sub-section, is incredibly tedious and requires many clicks. There is no bulk management tool for heavy users.
**Recommendation:** Introduce a desktop-style "Select Multiple" mode where users can tap multiple pins and drag them into folders or boards in a single action.

### 2. Unclear Link Destinations
**Heuristic Violated:** #2 Match between system and the real world
**Severity:** 2 (Minor usability problem)
**Description:** When viewing a Pin, it is not always immediately obvious if tapping the image will enlarge it, or if it will navigate away from the app to an external website.
**Recommendation:** Visually differentiate external links (e.g., overlay a small "external link" icon on the bottom right of the image) from images that merely expand within the app.
